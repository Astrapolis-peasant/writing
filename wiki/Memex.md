# Memex

Vannevar Bush's 1945 proposal for a personal, curated knowledge store connected by
associative trails between documents — cited as the direct ancestor of the LLM wiki
pattern ([[LLM Wiki (Karpathy)]]).

## Why it is invoked

Bush's vision was private, actively curated, and treated **the connections between
documents as being as valuable as the documents themselves**. Karpathy's argument is
that this is closer to an LLM wiki than to what the web actually became — the web
kept the linking but dropped the curation and the privacy.

## The unsolved piece

Bush had no answer for *who does the maintenance* of the trails. That is exactly the
cost an LLM drives to near zero, which is why the idea becomes practical eighty years
later. See [[Compounding Knowledge]].
