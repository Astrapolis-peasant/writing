# Psychic Vampire

[[Anton LaVey]]'s term for a person who extracts time, effort, and guilt from
others without ever making an explicit demand — the concrete social type behind
[[LaVeyan Satanism]]'s principle of "responsibility to the responsible."

The diagnostic is precise and, unusually for [[The Satanic Bible (LaVey)]],
falsifiable in individual cases: the obligation is never stated. "Their sins are
not of commission, but of omission. It's what they don't say, not what they do
say." The hint replaces the request; "Oh, I couldn't ask you to do that" produces
the offer. Gifts are given specifically so that repayment must come in
non-material form, which is to say indefinitely.

## The test

Weigh what you give against what you receive. Mutual dependence — a partner who
asks for your time and grants theirs, an employer who expects extra and returns
flexibility — is exchange, not predation. What marks the vampire is asymmetry:
someone who is unavailable whenever you need them, whose demands never reciprocate.

LaVey notes that the targets are selected for conscientiousness. The vampire picks
people with a strong sense of moral obligation, and often those who are visibly
content — precisely what the vampire lacks.

The prescribed remedy is refusal, delivered graciously, followed by the expectation
that the person will first act wounded and then turn vindictive. The book extends
the same analysis to organized fundraising, which it treats as the institutional
form of the technique.

## What it is worth

This is the part of the book that survives translation out of its framework. Strip
the vocabulary and it is an account of coercion through implied obligation, and of
altruism weaponized — the observation that a request one cannot decline without
appearing callous is a form of force.

The limitation is that the concept has no brake. Any request can be reframed as
vampirism, and a philosophy already committed to self-interest supplies no way to
tell exploitation from ordinary need. It offers a reason to refuse and no
corresponding test for when refusing is the wrong call.
